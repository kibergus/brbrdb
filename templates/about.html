Why

To better understand how my daughter progresses on her karting journey, how races unfold, and how she compares to other athletes, I built a couple of dashboards. And then somehow it grew into this site. I share it in the hope that it will help other athletes grow too, and in the belief that making knowledge accessible and structured is the right thing to do.

For me, this project is a hobby and a way to help my daughter; I do not seek any commercial gain from it. I don't mind if the whole system, or elements of it, make their way into other systems. I still need to clean up the code a bit and, after that, I will share it under Apache 2.0.

If you are the owner of one of the systems from which I ingest data, please don't see me as a competitor. In a sense, it would be easier for me if I could meet my needs using existing shared infrastructure. I only built this site because I think there are better ways to show the data.

Gallery

Some of you may have access to the gallery section of the site. This gallery is intended for sharing race photos with athletes or their guardians. It is then their decision whether to share these photos further (whether on social media or by sending them to family members). Please don't share access links widely; instead, share the photos themselves. As the author of the photos, I grant permission to use them for any lawful purposes, commercial or non-commercial, as long as you have permission to do so from the people in the photos or their guardians.

This gallery exists because it is easy for me to take a few extra photos while photographing my daughter on the track, and I hope it is useful and helps preserve memories. Thus, the gallery is semi-private, shared with people I meet at racing events.

You may find some videos in the gallery too. They will all be low-resolution, and currently I don't have a process for sharing them more widely without requiring a significant investment of my time.

Telemetry

Telemetry is an invaluable source of insights. It helps focus on specific aspects that can be improved. In a simulator, we can get a lot of detailed telemetry. I couldn't find an analysis platform that I liked, so I wrote my own. I also don't think that access to powerful data analysis should cost a fortune.

This feature is highly experimental and is not yet ready for general use, though I might start offering early access soon. Since I want to facilitate learning and help athletes grow, I will likely require the sessions to be publicly visible so that athletes can learn from each other.
