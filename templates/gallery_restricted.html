Gallery Access Restricted

The gallery is intended exclusively for athletes and their parents or guardians.

This gallery exists because it is easy for me to take a few extra photos while photographing my daughter on the track, and I hope it is useful and helps preserve memories. Race photos and videos are shared with athletes and their guardians so that they can decide whether to share them further.

If you are an athlete or a parent/guardian of an athlete taking part in the races and would like access to the gallery, find me on track or get in touch at brbrdb@kibergus.com.
